# Tariff Estimator for Amazon

## What This Tool Does

This browser add-on helps you see the hidden tariff costs when shopping on Amazon. When you view products made in China, it automatically shows you an estimate of how much of the price you're paying is actually a tariff (an import tax).

## Main Benefits

- **Finds Chinese Products:** Automatically identifies when a product is made in China.
- **Shows Hidden Costs:** Displays an estimate of the tariff amount right next to the product price.
- **Personalized Settings:** You can adjust the tariff rate and seller profit margin to match current policies or get more accurate estimates.
- **Remembers Your Settings:** Once you set your preferences, they'll be saved for future shopping sessions.
- **Clear Display:** Shows the information clearly, like "$20.99 (tariff est. $9.32)".
- **Works Everywhere:** Functions on all Amazon websites worldwide (.com, .co.uk, .ca, etc.).

## How It Calculates Tariffs

The tool uses a straightforward approach to estimate tariffs:

1. It understands that the price you see includes the original cost, the seller's profit, and the tariff.
2. Based on your settings, it works backward from the final price to figure out how much of that price is likely the tariff.

Note: This provides a simplified estimate and doesn't account for other factors like shipping costs, insurance, or currency conversion fees.

## How to Install

Since this extension isn't available in the Chrome Web Store yet, you'll need to install it manually:

1. **Get the Files:**
   - Click the green "Code" button and select "Download ZIP"
   - Once downloaded, unzip/extract the files to a folder on your computer (for example, to your Desktop or Documents folder)
2. **Open Chrome Extensions Page:** Open Google Chrome and type `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar, then press Enter.
3. **Turn On Developer Mode:** Find the "Developer mode" switch (usually in the top-right corner) and turn it ON.
4. **Add the Extension:** Click the "Load unpacked" button that appears.
5. **Find the Folder:** Browse to and select the folder containing the extension files.
6. **Complete:** The extension is now installed and ready to use.

## How to Use

1. **Set Your Preferences:** Click the extension's button in your Chrome toolbar. Enter your desired Tariff Rate and Seller Profit Margin percentages, then click "Save Settings". The default values are 145% for tariff rate and 25% for profit margin.
2. **Shop on Amazon:** Browse Amazon as you normally would.
3. **See the Estimates:** When viewing a product made in China, you'll automatically see the estimated tariff amount in parentheses next to the price. This updates if you select different product options or variants.
